One message, to the person it's for. It reads like it was written for them, because it was.

Why it matters

A shared channel is where work questions, incidents and announcements live. Every reply there pings everyone who follows it. One birthday wish costs nothing; sixty of them cost the whole team a few minutes of attention and push real messages out of sight.

It also does little for the person celebrating. A wall of "HBD" in a channel feels like a reflex. A short private note feels personal.

Simple rules

  • Send a direct message. Add one personal line if you can.
  • React instead of replying. If there's an announcement in the channel, a 🎉 reaction says the same thing without notifying anyone.
  • Use a thread. If your team likes to celebrate together, reply in a thread under the announcement so the channel stays readable.
  • Celebrate in person. Cake beats emoji, every time.
